Support

Back up your data

  • Open Settings › Backup › Back up data now.
  • Save the file to On My iPhone, not the Cycle folder inside it. Files in the Cycle folder are deleted if you delete the app.
  • Now and then, tap AirDrop backup file and send one to your computer, so your history survives a lost phone.

Restore a backup

  • Open Settings › Backup › Restore from backup, or tap Restore from a backup on the first screen after reinstalling.
  • Merge with my data adds what's in the backup and keeps everything already on your phone. Replace my data makes your data exactly the backup.
  • Either way, Cycle saves a safety copy of your current data first, in Files › On My iPhone › Cycle › Safety copies.

About predictions

  • Predictions are estimates from the dates you log, not medical advice. Don't use Cycle as birth control.
