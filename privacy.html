Privacy Policy

Cycle Period Tracker · effective October 28, 2026

Cycle doesn't collect your data

  • Everything you enter (period dates, symptoms, notes, weight and settings) is stored only on your iPhone.
  • Cycle has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. It doesn't connect to the internet.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself.

Backups

  • Backups are files you create and save where you choose, such as On My iPhone or a computer via AirDrop. Cycle can't see them anywhere else.

Reminders and lock

  • Reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.
  • Face ID and passcode checks are handled by iOS. Cycle never receives your biometric data.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.

Contact

  • Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
